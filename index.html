About

CREW — 001

Started editing as a hobby — cutting motivational videos to go with my own fitness content, long before it was a job. Somewhere between the first client and the fiftieth, the workflow tightened up: most projects now turn around in 1–2 days without losing the polish. The rule I still edit by — if I don't find it good to watch, I'm doing something wrong. Editing isn't just cutting clips together, it's making the video watchable, and shaping it around what the audience is actually going to watch.

My process stays the same no matter the project: rough cut first to lock the story, then transitions, then a full polish pass, then sound design — always last, never first. That order isn't random, it's what keeps the pacing honest. I work across marketing, gaming, fitness, and vlogs, which keeps me flexible instead of boxed into one style or one kind of client.
